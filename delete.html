Delete My Data

You can delete your Hanj account and all associated data at any time.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

Your email address and login credentials · Your anime lists (Watching, Completed, Plan to Watch, Dropped) · Episode progress, ratings, and notes · Taste profile and genre preferences · Activity feed history · Notification preferences · Founder status record (if applicable)

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

⚠ This cannot be reversed. Once deleted, your lists and progress cannot be recovered. Export or note anything you want to keep before proceeding.

How to delete from the app

  1. 1
    Open Hanj and make sure you are signed in to the account you want to delete.
  2. 2
    Tap Profile in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. 3
    Tap the ··· menu icon in the top-right corner of the Profile screen.
  4. 4
    Tap More to expand the additional options.
  5. 5
    Tap Delete Account and confirm when prompted.

Your account and all data will be permanently deleted immediately.

Can't access the app?

If you no longer have access to the app or your account, you can request manual deletion by contacting us directly:

Email us at hanj.app@gmail.com

Please include the email address associated with your account. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.
