Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: MAY 2026

Hanj is a personal anime tracking app built by an independent developer. This policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

When you create an account and use Hanj, we collect:

  • Email address and password — used to create and secure your account via Firebase Authentication.
  • Display name and profile photo — optional, set by you in your profile.
  • Anime list data — titles you add to Watching, Completed, Plan to Watch, and Dropped lists, along with episode progress, ratings, and notes.
  • Preferences and taste profile — genre preferences and viewing habits you set within the app.
  • Activity data — status changes and ratings that power the activity feed.
  • Notification preferences — your choices about which in-app notifications to receive.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and sync your anime tracking lists across sessions.
  • To personalise recommendations and the Pulse feed based on your list and genres.
  • To send episode reminders and airing notifications you have opted into.
  • To display your activity and stats within the app.

We do not sell your data. We do not serve ads. We do not share your personal information with third parties except as described below.

Third-Party Services

Hanj uses the following services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google LLC) — authentication, cloud database (Firestore), and push notifications (FCM). Firebase Privacy Policy →
  • AniList API — publicly available anime metadata (titles, scores, schedules). No personal data is sent to AniList. AniList Terms →
  • YouTube (Google LLC) — trailers are played via the YouTube player. Viewing may be subject to YouTube's privacy policy. Google Privacy Policy →

Data Retention

Your data is stored in Firebase Firestore for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from the app (Profile → ··· menu → Delete Account), which permanently removes all your data from our servers.

Children's Privacy

Hanj is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To delete your account and all associated data, see the Delete My Data page. For other requests, contact us at the email below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of Hanj after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
